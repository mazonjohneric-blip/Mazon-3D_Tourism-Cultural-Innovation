A different kind of getaway

Slow down.
Look a little closer.

Set in the Mimaropa region, Marinduque brings together laid-back island days and a culture with deep roots. Follow a winding road to a quiet beach, wander through the heritage streets of Boac, or simply let the afternoon unfold.

It’s a place made for curious travelers: full of natural beauty, welcoming communities, and moments you’ll want to carry home.

Three ways to wander

Find your Marinduque

01 / By the water

Island time

Let the sea set the pace. Seek out the sandy shoreline of Poctoy White Beach or take a boat toward the Tres Reyes Islands.

02 / Out in nature

Follow the outdoors

Trade the shoreline for forest paths, cool waterfalls, and the quiet wonder of Bathala Caves. Leave every place as you found it.

03 / Around town

Stories in the streets

Take your time in Boac, the provincial capital, and discover its historic center and the centuries-old Boac Cathedral.

A tradition with heart

Meet the spirit of Moriones.

Each Holy Week, the Moriones Festival brings masked figures in Roman-inspired costumes to the streets of Marinduque. Rooted in local faith and storytelling, this vivid tradition is one of the island’s most beloved cultural celebrations.
